Our Journey

How love found us

✦

In their own words.

01
From the groom

How we met & fell in love

It started with an introduction through a childhood friend that turned into phone calls, leading to our very first meeting. We spent six hours talking over food, completely lost in conversation. I knew right away I liked what I saw even if she still playfully debates who fell first.

From traveling together for weddings to simple everyday moments, we grew closer with every step. She is brilliant, selfless, and deeply empathetic, rooted in her faith and acknowledgement of God. Where she moves fast and gets things done, I like to take my time; our strengths balance each other, making us a complete team.

Looking back at where it all began, I know I found my person. If I had to choose all over again in another life, I'd choose you every time.

02
From the bride

How we met

Introduced by a mutual friend, we started talking and instantly clicked. I fell in love with his intelligence, his deep kindness, his love for God and how effortlessly he understands humor. More than anything, his steady nature brings a perfect calm to my passionate, fiery heart. We balance each other in all the best ways, and we can't wait to begin this next chapter together!
